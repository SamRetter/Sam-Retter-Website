The approach

Good projects get complicated long before the cameras roll.

Heartwood Pictures works alongside independent producers on the financial and commercial questions that sit between a creative idea and a sustainable production. From development economics and greenlight decisions through all stages of financing, production and project stewardship.

The work

Keep the project coherent as it grows.

01

Develop & decide

Development economics, greenlight models, budgets, incentive assumptions, finance plans and commercial scenarios.

02

Finance & structure

Production cashflow, financing routes, co-production structures, partner terms, funding gaps and funding flows.

03

Keep the project connected

Adviser coordination, reporting, incentive and audit coordination, stakeholder requirements and project / SPV stewardship.

04

Build the company around it

Slate forecasting, cash runway, management reporting, controls and strategic finance support.

UK / New Zealand

Rooted locally. Built to travel.

UK-based with Kiwi roots, Heartwood Pictures has a particular interest in projects moving between the two markets. The focus is practical: make the financial model, structure, assumptions and obligations legible on both sides, while working alongside the right producers, financiers, accountants, lawyers and production teams.
